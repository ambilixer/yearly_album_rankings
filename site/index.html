About this data

Where it comes from. Star ratings are mine, from Rate Your Music, out of 5. Every so often I export my RYM collection and rank every rated release from the year by hand. Rank always respects stars: nothing rated 3.5 can sit above anything rated 4.0. Any release type counts (LP, EP, mixtape, compilation).

Drift. I sometimes re-evaluate an album while ranking, so its stars can change between updates, and can differ from what RYM showed on the export date.

Badges. ▲/▼ is places gained or lost since the previous update. New means on the list for the first time; Back means it returned after dropping off. Hot debut: arrived within the last two updates and is already top 10. Surging / Slipping: passed (or was passed by) 5+ albums that were already on the list, over the last three updates, and still heading that way. New arrivals landing above an album don't count against it. Staple: top 10 in every update since it arrived, across at least four.

The queue. Releases in my RYM collection from this year that I haven't rated. The artist note is my average RYM rating of that artist's other releases, matched by name.

Covers. Where no cover art is on file yet, a placeholder tile stands in.
