About this data

Where it comes from. Star ratings are mine, from Rate Your Music, out of 5. At each snapshot I export my RYM collection and rank every rated release from the year by hand. Rank always respects stars: nothing rated 3.5 can sit above anything rated 4.0. Any release type counts (LP, EP, mixtape, compilation).

Stars can differ from RYM. The stars shown for a snapshot are the ones I gave while ranking. I sometimes re-evaluate an album while ranking, so these can differ from what RYM had on the export date.

Badges. ▲/▼ is places gained or lost since the previous snapshot. New means first time ranked; Back means it returned after dropping out. Hot debut: first ranked within the last two snapshots and now top 10. Surging / Slipping: passed (or was passed by) 5+ albums that were already ranked, over the last three snapshots, and still heading that way. New entries landing above an album don't count against it. Staple: top 10 in every snapshot since it debuted, across at least four.

The queue. Releases in my RYM collection from this year that I haven't rated. The artist note is my average RYM rating of that artist's other releases, matched by name.

Covers. Where no cover art is on file, a placeholder tile is generated from the album's ID.
